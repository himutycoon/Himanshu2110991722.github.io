Subscriptions (SaaS and AI Chat Assistant)

  • Cancel any time by emailing codeverasolutions@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp. No cancellation fee.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. The service stays active until the end of the period already paid for.
  • Payments for a period that has already started are not refunded, except as listed below.

Downloadable products (plugins and tools)

If a product does not work as described and we cannot fix it within 7 days of your report, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

Custom development projects

Milestone payments cover work already started or delivered and are not refundable once that milestone's work has begun. If we cancel a project, we refund any payment for work not yet started.

When we always refund

  • Duplicate or accidental charges.
  • Charges made after a cancellation was confirmed.
  • A paid service we were unable to deliver.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
